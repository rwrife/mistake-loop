Mistake Loop is a private, offline study mistake journal for errors you want to understand, not hide. Record your wrong answer, corrected answer, source locator, and error causes across subjects, then revisit unresolved questions in a dedicated self-marking queue.

Built for Windows, Android phones and tablets, and iPhone and iPad with 100% local SQLite storage, versioned JSON backup and restore, and CSV export.

Key Features:
- Offline study journal: keep your own correction trail private on your device.
- Focused revisit queue: write a fresh answer before revealing the correction; mark resolved only after a successful attempt, and reopen mistakes anytime.
- Subject and cause organization: categorize errors by subject and tag specific causes (calculation error, sign mistake, misread question, etc.).
- Platform-authentic interfaces: Cupertino styling on iOS/iPadOS, Material 3 on Android, and Fluent-style navigation on Windows desktop.
- Privacy-first portability: export/import versioned JSON backups and export CSV summaries through your platform's native file picker.
- Zero tracking: no account required, no ads, no telemetry, no AI grading claims, and no copied copyrighted question banks.
